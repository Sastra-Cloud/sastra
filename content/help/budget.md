Open the project’s **Budget** tab. The current scope's quotation, committed funding,
and funding gap stay visible above the content tabs. **Quotation** is selected
first. Managers can choose **Partners & proposals** for partner shares and emails. Choose **Approvals**, **Cash flow**, **Payments**, or **Notes** to show
that section directly. Tabs use a short fade and a sliding indicator. The page is
only as long as the selected tab, and only that tab is interactive. If you have
scrolled down, choosing another tab shows it from its top. Reduced-motion
preferences skip the animation.
Switching tabs retains unsaved fields. Bookmarks and
exception links select the matching tab, including links to a payment or field.
Errors reveal their section. **Budget steps** under the Budget heading shows
what is done and what to do next. Main, active reprint, and all-history scopes keep their existing records;
the read-only all-history view has no Approvals tab.

The Budget separates the real **Project costs** from the **Partner quote** a
donor or funding partner sees and pays. Managers can present selected itemized
costs or one price per copy. Internal labels, notes, spending, and the
organization donation fee are never included in a partner-facing file.

At the top, **Budget attention** lists the current exceptions first: approval
changes or pending decisions, funding still remaining, overdue receivables, and
reconciliation issues. **Next** jumps to the highest-priority item. This is a
summary of the same quotation and payment records shown below; it does not
approve, send, or change anything.

Quotation lines, funding receipts, and payment status update immediately while
they save. If the server rejects a change—for example because a receipt is
locked to an expected payment—the previous value returns and the editor
keeps the attempted values for correction.
Receipts created from the admin **Donations** ledger are also locked on the
project Budget page. An admin corrects their project split from **Donations** so
the original gift, unallocated remainder, and review history stay together.
An admin can choose **Link to expected payment** beneath an imported donation receipt.
Choose an unpaid payment for the same project, print run, and currency. Review
the expected amount, the amount already received, and their difference.
If the amounts differ, explain why this completes the scheduled payment.
Choose **Confirm as received** only when the payment is complete. Leave a part
payment open.

You do not need an invoice to link a payment. You can also choose an expected
payment that has **no amount yet**. Sastra then saves the amount received as its
amount and marks it received. Check that this is the full payment first.

This links the existing receipt and marks the payment received without adding
money a second time.
The original expected amount, received amount, and available net amount stay unchanged.
The payment schedule shows the actual received amount when it differs. Extra
funding stays in the project totals. Correct donation-linked payments from
**Donations**, where their review history is kept.
Proposal status changes and **Adopt recorded** reconciliation actions follow the
same pattern: the badge or mismatch row changes immediately, then returns if the
save fails.

For a printer estimate, expand **Estimated printing cost** and choose **Request
estimate**. Set width, height, and units in the request form. **Same specs as…**
searches books with saved production specifications and copies size, paper,
cover finish, and binding. All copied fields remain editable. Page count,
quantities, and other notes stay specific to this request. Preparing the email
still does not send it.

## Offer different print quantities to a partner

In an itemized **Partner quote**, the available printer quantities appear
under **Printing options by quantity**. Review the copies, cost, and specifications
against the printer email. Choose **Add all … quantities** to include every
quantity in the partner quote, or **Add …-copy option** to include them individually.
Use **Add more quantities** when the quote already has options. You can use proposal estimates before the book is ready to print.
Quotes must use the budget currency; the app does not convert currencies.

The editor separates **Shared project costs** from printing options. The shared
costs apply to every quantity. Each option shows its printing price and the
**Project price** for the shared costs plus that one quantity. These are
alternatives that all appear in the quote. The partner chooses one quantity
to fund; the options are never added together. The
ordinary Printing / Shipping budget line is excluded from the partner quote
while options are included, so printing is not counted twice. Include any
shipping needed for an option in its reviewed printing price.

Edit the partner description and printing price for each option. The initial
project price covers the shared internal costs and that quantity’s printing cost
after the organization donation fee is retained. For example, with a 13% fee,
the funding target is the full internal cost divided by 0.87, rounded up to a cent.
Each row shows the organization fee amount, funding available after the fee, and
whether that quantity covers costs.

After changing the fee percentage or shared costs, choose **Apply …% fee
coverage** in the coverage box below the printing options. It recalculates each
option independently and saves the quote. If shared lines are marked **adjust**,
the same action sets their rates first, then prices every quantity against those
rates, so the partner pays the fee once. Prices you type yourself stay visible
drafts until you choose **Save partner quote**; all included quantities appear in
the partner file. The fee calculation stays internal.

Options appear immediately as a draft. Use **Save partner quote** before
sharing. A failed save keeps your edits for correction and retry. Removing an
option removes it from the partner quote only. Adding an option never accepts
a printer quote, changes the selected budget estimate, or creates a payment.
Saved options retain the reviewed quantity and printer cost when the source
quote changes later; remove and add an option again to use a revised quote.

The partner spreadsheet and proposal attachment label the non-printing subtotal
**Project costs before printing**. Separate quantity rows show **Copies**,
**Per copy** (printing only), **Printing price**, and **Project price**. Per copy
is the printing price divided by copies, rounded for display. Each project price
adds the non-printing subtotal to that quantity’s printing price; options are never
summed. The project title appears once, with any distinct work description kept
beneath it. Internal printer costs, printer
identity, source emails, and the organization fee stay out of the partner file.
The budget summary shows **Partner quotation from**: the lowest project price
among the options. Approval and proposal history records that need one amount
also use this minimum; approval covers the complete set of saved options.
Agree the quantity with the partner before setting the payment schedule.
For **One price per copy**, the existing single-quantity PDF is used; the
printing options are retained but inactive until you return to itemized costs.

## Budget steps

**Budget steps** is a short list at the top of the main project Budget. It reads
the project’s saved records, so it is always up to date. Each step is done,
next, or later. The **next** step says what is missing and has one button that
opens the right place.

1. **Book size.** The word count or the pages of the original book. Sastra uses
   it to calculate word and page costs. This step appears when a cost needs it.
2. **Costs.** What the work will cost. A cost with no amount keeps this step
   open.
3. **Printing cost.** For books. Ask a printer for an estimate, or wait for the
   printer’s price from the Print tab. For other kinds of project this step is
   optional.
4. **Partner quote.** The price the funding partner sees. The step tells you
   when the quote is less than the costs need after the organization fee.
5. **Proposal.** Start a proposal for a partner. If your team must approve the
   budget first, the step says so and opens **Approvals**.
6. **Answer and money.** Record what the partner decides, then add the expected
   payments.

Choose **Hide steps** if you do not need the list. **Show steps** brings it
back. The list also follows **Settings ▸ Profile ▸ Show helpful guidance**.
Steps never send, approve, or change anything by themselves.

## Guided budget setup

A main project budget with no costs opens **Set up the budget**. It has four
short steps:

1. **Book size.** Enter the word count and the pages of the original book. Leave
   them empty if you do not know yet; costs that need them wait.
2. **Costs.** Tick the costs this project has. Rates come from your workspace
   settings. Each row shows quantity, rate, and amount as you type. Audiobook
   and video start unticked. Choose **Add custom cost** for anything else.
   Printing is not in this list: it comes from a printer estimate.
3. **Funding partner.** Choose a saved partner, type a new one, or choose
   **Decide later**. Add the contact email here, because you need it to send a
   proposal. Sastra saves a new partner in **Partners**.
4. **Review.** Check the book size, costs, and partner, then choose **Create
   budget**. Nothing is emailed. The screen then moves to the new costs under
   **Main budget plan** and tints them green for a moment.

Your entries stay in the form if saving fails. **Skip guided setup** shows the
older choices: **Add standard budget items** or **Add custom line**. **Use guided
setup** brings the steps back while the budget still has no costs.

## Budget in four steps

New to budgets? Work through these tabs:

1. **Plan the costs.** In **Quotation**, list what the work will cost. Then set
   the partner quote — the price the funding partner sees.
2. **Get approval and send the proposal.** In **Approvals**, ask your team to
   approve the quote (this is optional). In **Partners & proposals**, build and send each partner’s proposal.
   When the partner replies, choose **Record answer**.
3. **Track the money.** In **Cash flow**, record funding as it
   arrives and check that it matches the plan.
4. **Handle agreements and fees.** In **Payments**, manage expected
   payments from partners, royalties, and license fees.

Marking a royalty payment paid completes its linked task; undoing the paid mark
reopens it. Managers can reassign the task, which also changes the payment
owner. A linked task points back to its payment, where the paid state is changed.

You can open any section at any time. Nothing is sent or changed until you
approve it.

The **Cash flow** summary shows received, paid out, and net cash first. Monthly
charts appear after there are at least two months of activity, avoiding empty
chart space when a project has only one recorded month.

## Proposal budgets before committing to a project

On **Projects**, choose **New proposal budget** to cost a book or project still
under consideration. Start with a working title and estimated word/page counts,
select standard costs at workspace rates, or add custom costs. Rates support four
decimal places, so per-word prices stay accurate after saving and recalculation.
Unknown word/page quantities can stay blank; the total warns that those costs are
incomplete. **Save proposal budget** saves it as a Proposal project and opens Budget.
It creates no tasks, funding, deadlines, or Lectio counterpart, and sends no email.

Refine the budget and partner quotation here. Once approved, change the same
project to Planning or Active; the existing estimate stays attached.

The project's overview now has a **Project funding** section with available net
funds after recorded spend, received payments and donations, and outstanding
promises. Shared MoU/grant allocations, expected payments, and promised
donations link to their records. Gross promises and received cash stay distinct; currencies, reprints,
and sponsorship balances are not combined. This finance section is available to
workspace managers and admins.

## Building the quotation

### Invoicing one part of an existing project

In **Payments**, open **Invoice selected budget lines** and choose the costs
the partner will cover, such as **Audiobook Production**. The invoice uses only
those lines' partner-safe labels and saved partner prices. It stays on the same
project. A budget line can be invoiced even when the main quotation uses one
price per copy.

Leave **Invoice amount** blank for the full selected cost, or enter a smaller
contribution. Review the partner name, email, billing address, amount, and payment
date, then confirm **Create invoice**. You can use another partner for this
invoice without changing the project's main partner. This creates a separate
scheduled payment and an invoice PDF. It does not record funding received or
email anyone. Use **Email invoice** on the payment to review the message, CC
list, and attachment, then confirm the send separately.

Existing project funding is not subtracted automatically: it may cover different
work. Lines with a linked invoice show a reminder to check existing payments
before adding another. If PDF creation fails, retry the same request to finish
the existing payment rather than creating another one. A budget or partner
change between review and creation requires a fresh review.

While viewing a project, you can ask the **Sastra Assistant**:
“Create an invoice for this book's audiobook production.” It reads the live
budget and existing invoices first, then shows the covered work, public amount,
and partner details for approval. An existing matching invoice should be reused.
It never creates another project to represent part of a book. If a cost is
missing, it can propose adding a budget line using the quantity and rate you
supply; humans can use **Add custom line** in **Quotation**. Both changes require
manager or admin access. Invoice creation and email sending are separate actions.

Managers and admins can also ask the **Sastra Assistant** to create a complete
new project from supplied planning data. When the request includes a deliverable
count, partner, word count, internal costs, and public rates, the assistant
shows a reviewable project blueprint before creating the project, workflow,
budget lines, and partner quote together. Use the normal Budget page to adjust
the saved lines afterward. These conversational budget projects start in
**Proposal** status by default and can be moved to Planning or Active after the
partner approves the work.

While viewing an existing project, managers can also ask the assistant when the
project could realistically start or finish. It uses the same live, path-aware
capacity model as the completion planner. After you choose a date, the assistant
can propose updating the real **Proposed completion date** in the funding
proposal; the change is not saved until you approve it. A signed agreement's
completion deadline remains authoritative and cannot be replaced this way.

1. Click **Add standard budget items** to create the standard publishing lines. You
   don't have to seed first — **Add custom line** works on an empty budget too,
   if you'd rather build the quotation by hand.
2. Edit the **Qty** on any standard word-based line, such as Translation. That
   inline value is the shared **Project word count**, so pressing Enter or
   leaving the field updates every word-driven line (translation, editing,
   proofreading, audiobook, and video) together. **Typesetting** also
   recalculates from the word count when no source page count is set. You can
   still edit the same project value in **Quotation settings**. Changes appear
   immediately while saving and roll back if saving fails.
3. Adjust any **unit price** inline, or **reset** it to the default rate. Use
   **Add custom line** for one-off costs. Managers can also edit a line’s name,
   quantity, unit price, assigned funding, and amount spent. A custom line’s unit
   can be changed after it is created.
4. Use **Funding assigned** when promised money is earmarked for a specific
   line. Received donations appear in the project totals automatically; you do
   not need to assign them to every line.

The summary strip shows **Project costs · Partner quote · Committed · Received ·
Available · Spent**. **Committed** includes expected payments from a partner, with or without an
MoU, even before an invoice is paid. It also includes a donation a partner
promised but has not given yet; **Received** includes only
gross money recorded in the receipt ledger. **Available** is the actual net
amount left after the organization donation fee; older receipts without net
data use the scope’s snapshotted fee to estimate the available amount. The same agreement is not
double-counted when its commitment also appears in line-level **Funding
assigned** values.
Budget group headings follow the project format: for example, article projects
show **Article Publishing**, while video and podcast media groups use their
production labels. The underlying cost categories and calculations are
unchanged.
When a partner quote uses an organization donation fee, its summary card also
shows the rate and **expected net** after that fee. This is a forecast; the separate
**Available** card remains actual received funding available to spend.

## Partner quote and organization donation fee

Open **Partner quote** and choose:

- **Itemized costs** to show selected public lines. Edit the partner-safe label
  and rate without changing the internal cost. **Apply …% fee coverage**
  suggests upward-rounded public rates on the lines marked **adjust**.
- **One price per copy** to show only quantity, price per copy, and total.
  **Suggest price** rounds upward to the nearest cent needed to cover costs.

Checking or unchecking a partner line updates its visibility and quotation
totals immediately. Only that line pauses while its change saves; you can
continue choosing other lines. If saving fails, that line returns to its
previous state and an error explains that you can try again. Other successful
changes stay in place. Unfinished label and rate edits stay in their fields
during other saves. **Download Excel** (itemized) or **Download PDF** (per copy)
becomes available after saving finishes.

The **Organization donation fee** is the percentage your organization retains
from every donation. It is saved per budget scope. New projects and reprints
snapshot the current workspace default. Existing budgets stay unchanged until a
manager clicks **Set up partner quote**. The coverage sentence shows partner
total, expected available funding, and internal costs. Undercoverage is a
warning, not a send blocker.

The **Main budget plan** and its internal cost table appear before the
partner-facing quotation controls, so managers can establish the real delivery
cost first. Below the internal cost table, Sastra shows **Project costs**, the
system-calculated **Organization fee coverage**, and the **Minimum funding
target**. The coverage is calculated from the gross donation—not by simply
adding 13% to the costs—so the full project cost remains after the organization
fee.
It is internal only, cannot be edited as an expense, and is never shown as a
fee line to the partner.

For an itemized partner quote, mark **adjust** on the public lines that should
absorb the funding difference. Then choose **Apply …% fee coverage** in the
coverage box under the quote. There is one button for the whole quote: it sets
the rates of the marked lines, prices every printing quantity, and saves. Sastra
uses every internal cost, including hidden shipping or handling, when it
calculates the selected public rates. This changes only the partner-safe rates;
the real internal costs remain unchanged. Suggested rates are calculated from
the saved internal amounts and rounded upward at the displayed rate precision;
Sastra verifies that the resulting expected net is at least the full internal
cost, including when a line amount was adjusted separately from its unit rate.

You can apply fee coverage straight after changing the organization donation
fee. Sastra saves the open quote settings first, because the rates are
calculated from the saved fee. If no line is marked **adjust** and the quote has
no printing quantities, the button is off and the box says what to do.

After applying, **Undo fee coverage** appears beside the Apply button. It
restores the previous partner rates for the lines that changed, including rates
that inherited their internal cost, and the previous printing prices. It does
not change the fee. Rates and totals update immediately while Apply or Undo
saves; a failed save restores the previous view and shows an error. Undo cannot
overwrite a rate edited since coverage was applied. Finish unfinished line edits
before using either button, and save other quote changes before Undo. Undo is
available for the last application while this budget remains open. Reloading
or leaving the page clears it. Internal costs, labels, visibility, and other lines are preserved.

When the partner quote has no unsaved changes, its action area shows **Saved**
instead of prompting you to save again. Editing the quote brings back **Save
partner quote**; preview remains tied to the last saved version until the change
is saved. Saving also works when the app was updated after you opened the quote.
You do not need to reload and lose your description. If a save fails, the quote
keeps your edits so you can try again. **Saved** appears only after the server
confirms the save.

**Export to Excel** is the internal detailed workbook. **Preview partner file**
creates a sanitized Excel workbook for itemized quotes or a PDF for per-copy
quotes. The file downloads while your current budget page stays open. Invoice
links open an inline preview of the document, where you can download it.

## Set up a reprint budget

Choose the active reprint under **Budget scope**. When the scoped budget is
empty, **Next: Add accepted print quote** jumps to its setup card. If the Print
tab already has an accepted quote, click **Add accepted quote to budget** to
create the reprint’s Print / ship line using the accepted total; you do not need
to retype it. Add freight, insurance, local delivery, or other costs with **Add
a custom reprint cost**.

New custom lines and accepted-quote lines appear immediately while the server
saves them. Existing lines update immediately after **Save**. A small
**Saving…** label identifies the row in flight; other rows remain available. If
the save fails, Sastra removes the temporary line or restores the previous
saved totals, reopens the attempted values for correction, and shows the error.

The project quotation is your **fundraising plan** — what to raise. The Print
tab remains the operational source for printer quotes, invoices, and payments;
the run-scoped Budget view itemizes the accepted reprint cost and any additional
costs. **AI operating cost** in Notes is shown as an operational cost and is deliberately *not*
part of the quotation.

## Moving between budget sections

Use the tabs to switch between **Quotation**, **Approvals**, **Cash flow**,
**Payments**, and **Notes**. One section is visible at a time; its editors stay
mounted, so switching tabs does not discard drafts. On mobile, scroll the tab
row to reach another section. The selected tab stays visible. Pending approval
and cash issues show counts on their tabs, and urgent issues remain above them.

Existing section bookmarks keep working. For example, `#operations-notes`
selects Notes and `#cash-reconciliation` selects Cash flow. Changing tabs changes
only the view: it does not approve, complete, send, or dismiss anything.

On books with more than one print run, **Budget scope** switches between **Main
project**, each reprint, and **All history**. Main and reprint finances remain
separate. All history is an aggregate, read-only audit view.

**Expected payments** lists money a partner promised for a set amount. The
promise can come from an MoU or from an agreement without an MoU. Each payment
shows **MoU** or **No MoU** when this is recorded, and the partner’s name when
it came from a proposal. The first payment is due **On signing** for an MoU and
**On agreement** without one. Older payments show neither label until you edit
them.

Choose **Add expected payment** to add one by hand. Set **Agreement** to MoU
or No MoU. When the project has sent proposals, **Proposal** links the payment
to the partner’s proposal.

The main Budget starts with **Funding summary**, using the same figures as
Project overview and Rights & MOUs. It separates promised funding, gross money
received, the confirmed unpaid balance, and funds available after deductions and
spend. Missing installment amounts and unlinked receipts show **Needs review**
instead of a misleading unpaid balance. Budget attention asks for funding review
before treating that incomplete schedule as a funding gap or overdue debt.

**Review payments** opens Payments, and **Review received funding** opens Cash
flow. Save missing amounts first, then link earlier receipts to the right
installments. An admin can use **Link to expected payment** on a recorded donation;
this marks the installment received without adding another receipt. If admin
verification is needed, it returns you to the Budget receipt section. Next, review
and invoice the unpaid installment.

A legacy row described as payable to the licensor offers **Review fee** rather
than an incoming invoice. Review it as an outgoing rights cost. Its saved record
is kept until a manager corrects it.

When payment terms state a percentage, Sastra calculates missing installment
amounts from the stated total. Separate fixed fees stay separate. Existing saved
amounts are kept. If the payment terms have no total, Sastra can suggest an
amount from the current quotation in the same currency. Conflicting totals need
review.

An older row with no saved amount shows the suggested amount and calculation.
Choose **Review & invoice** to review it in place. **Calculate a percentage**
shows editable **Percentage** and **Total** fields; changing either updates the
expected amount. Check the total against the agreement before saving. You can
also enter the expected amount directly.
The form also shows received funding that is not linked to this payment
schedule. Check those receipts so an earlier payment is not invoiced again.

Choose **Save & generate invoice** to save the reviewed payment amount and
prepare its invoice PDF. The form stays open while preparing. If invoice
preparation fails after saving, the amount stays saved and the form keeps your
values for retry. Review the PDF, then use **Review & send** to review and send
the email separately.

Managers can edit an **unpaid** expected payment from **Edit payment** on its row.
The amount, trigger, expected date, agreement, proposal, partner invoice
description, internal note, and invoice owner are editable.
Without an invoice, the row updates immediately while saving and returns to the
previous state with the entered values reopened if saving fails. **Edit payment**
opens one form for all these fields. For an **unsent
invoice generated by Sastra**, it also includes **Invoice date** and **Invoice due
date**. The expected date is a planning date; the invoice dates appear on the PDF.
Choose **Save payment & refresh invoice** to save the payment, invoice, and linked
task together under the same invoice number. If preparing the PDF or saving fails,
the previous payment and invoice remain intact. Sent or imported invoices require
the existing void-and-replacement workflow for financial changes. Old PDFs remain
in history.
Received payments are locked to
protect their receipt history; choose
**Undo received payment** first when a correction is genuinely required.

Choose **Split partner total into 2** to create equal initial and final
installments; any odd cent goes to the final installment. Generating an invoice
stores a PDF with one partner-safe line and the workspace remittance
instructions. **Mark received** opens a review step for gross amount, actual
available net, and received date.

When a project belongs to a **Shared MoU**, managers see its reviewed agreement
share near the budget summary. The group's payment schedule is not duplicated on
each project. After a shared payment is recorded once, this project receives its
proportional funding allocation as a receipt; all project allocations reconcile
exactly to the group receipt. Open the Shared MoU banner for the collective
completion gate, full schedule, invoice, and receipt reconciliation.

### Record a past paid invoice

For an unpaid row in **Expected payments**, choose **Upload paid invoice** and
select the original PDF, image, or Word file. AI reads the invoice number, dates,
bill-to details, description, currency, and total. Review every field, confirm
that your organization issued the invoice, select the scheduled payment it
settles, and enter the actual payment-received date.

Nothing is recorded during extraction. **Save invoice & record receipt** stores
the original document, saves or updates the invoice, adjusts the selected
schedule to the reviewed amount when necessary, and records the funding receipt
together. If any part fails, neither the invoice nor receipt is left half-saved.
The same flow is available through the project header's **Update from document**
button when AI detects that the uploaded document is an invoice.

## Multiple partners for one project

Managers can open **Partners & proposals** and choose **Start a proposal**.
Keep the complete cost plan in **Quotation**. Each partner quotation uses only
that partner’s share; it does not create another project or duplicate costs.

**Start a proposal** has three short steps:

1. **Partner.** The project’s funding partner is already filled in. Change it,
   or choose another saved partner. Add the contact email here.
2. **What to ask for.** Every cost with an amount left to assign is already selected.
   Untick a cost, or lower an amount, to ask this partner for less. Sastra
   writes the title for you; you can change it.
3. **Review.** Choose **Save and write the email**. Sastra saves the partner
   quotation and opens the proposal email for you to check. Nothing is sent
   until you review it and choose **Send proposal**.

Choose **Show the full form** to see every field at once. Editing a saved
partner quotation with the pencil button always uses the full form, which works
like this:

1. Leave the partner blank to record a funding need, or choose a saved partner or enter their name and contact details. Unassigned quotations show **Partner needed**.
2. Give the quotation a clear title, such as “Translation and layout”.
3. Select the costs they will cover. Each row shows the amount still available.
4. To share a cost, enter only this partner’s amount. Another partner can cover
   the rest. Shares cannot exceed the visible cost or printing price.
5. Add an optional description and choose **Save quotation**.

When you save a quotation with a new partner name, Sastra adds the partner and
entered contact details to **Settings → Partners**. You can select them on future
quotations. Matching names reuse the existing partner; matching contact emails
reuse that partner’s contact. Changing the partner name links the quotation to
the matching or newly added partner, without renaming the previous directory entry.

Saved quotations reserve their assigned amounts, including drafts that have not
been sent. A cost with no amount left shows **Fully assigned to saved quotations**.
Under each assigned cost, Sastra shows the quotation title, partner, and amount
already assigned. Choose **Edit quotation** there to open that saved quotation;
Sastra asks before closing your current form without saving it. Reuse the existing
quotation to change its printing options, or reduce its share before assigning
that amount to a new quotation. **Assigned** does not mean funded or received.

Add another quotation for audio production, printing, or other costs. The summary
shows costs to share, amounts assigned to quotations, and amounts still to assign.
These are planned requests, not committed funding or money received. Record the
agreed funding in **Payments** after the partner commits. Quotations do not create
payments or mark any cost as funded. Several quotations can use the same partner.

Partner shares use the public labels and rates of visible itemized costs. Review
those rates first, including any funding target needed to cover the organization
fee. Printing quantity options saved in the itemized project quotation also appear
in the share form. Select any quantities you want the partner to compare; each
can be split between quotations. They replace the ordinary Printing / Shipping
line. The partner chooses one quantity, so alternative prices are never added
together. Totals show **From** the lowest included option, and each saved card
and attachment lists the complete price for each quantity. Per-copy presentations
remain in the original full-project quotation.
Main-project and reprint quotations stay separate. This manager tool is unavailable
in the read-only **All history** view.

Each saved card lists its title, partner or **Partner needed**, included work, and total.
Add a partner name and contact email before sending; an unassigned quotation can
be saved, downloaded, and submitted for internal approval.
**Download quotation** exports only this share. Open **Review proposal and send
history** to draft an email. The recipient and attachment come from this saved
quotation. Review the recipient, CC list, message, and attachment, then explicitly
confirm before choosing **Send proposal**. Each quotation has its own saved email
draft and sent history. Other partners’ names, amounts, and private budget notes
are excluded from the attachment.

Each saved quotation has an optional **Proposal approval** panel. Select another
active manager or admin and a shared due date, then choose **Request approval**.
Every selected person receives a protected review task and notification. They
can approve or request changes from the quotation or their task. Once requested,
all selected approvers must approve before that proposal can be sent. Existing
whole-budget approval requirements still apply. Editing the quotation or its
source project costs requires fresh approval; another partner’s independent
share does not reset this proposal’s own approval. Previous rounds remain in
its approval history. Assigning a partner after approval changes the quotation
and requires the reviewers to approve that version before sending.

Reviewers can open their approval task in **My Work**, or follow the approval
notification to the saved quotation. Choose **Review proposal and attachment**
in the task to see the partner, included costs, currency, and print quantity
options. On the quotation, **Your approval is needed** marks the review awaiting
your decision. **Review quotation** and **Download quotation** keep the plan
and its attachment within reach. With print alternatives, the amount starts at
**From** the lowest quantity; review every included quantity.

Choose **Approve proposal** when the quotation is ready, or **Request changes**
and explain **What needs to change?** before choosing **Send change request**.
Approval does not send the email. Every reviewer must approve before the sender
can send it. A change request pauses sending until the requester revises the
quotation and the reviewers approve again. The panel shows how many reviewers
have approved and which decision is yours. A failed decision keeps your note
and offers another attempt.

Saving updates the visible quotation while the request runs. Failed saves restore
the previous card and keep the form for correction. Deletion asks for confirmation,
then removes the card immediately; failure restores it. Sent proposals and their
exact attachments stay in history after deletion. Older proposals are shown under
**Original full-project proposal and history**.

While AI writes the proposal email, a spinner and **Writing your proposal email…**
message show that the draft is being prepared. When it finishes, review the
recipient, message, and attachment. If drafting fails, you can try again.

Changes to the project quotation mark saved shares **Needs review**. Edit and save
each affected share before exporting or sending again. Saved partner quotations
are included in the project’s approval review. Adding, changing, or deleting a
share resets any required approval. Once partner shares exist, use those proposals;
the original full-project proposal remains available as history.

## Record the partner’s answer

A proposal does not always become an MoU. In **Partners & proposals**, the
**Proposals and answers** card shows one row for each sent proposal: the
partner, the amount **Asked**, the partner’s answer, the amount **Promised**,
and the amount **Received**. Each row names the next step.

When the partner replies, choose **Record answer** and pick one:

- **They will sign an MoU.** Enter the agreed amount. Sastra can add the
  expected payments: half on signing and half on completion, or one payment.
  Use **Track MoU signing** to open **Rights & MOUs**.
- **They agreed without an MoU.** The partner agreed by email or in
  conversation. Enter the agreed amount. Sastra can add the expected payments:
  half on agreement and half on completion, or one payment. You can still send
  an invoice for each payment.
- **They will give a donation.** No MoU and no invoice. Enter the promised
  amount if you know it, or leave it blank. A promised amount counts as
  committed funding until the money arrives. If the money is already on the
  project, tick it to link it to this proposal.
- **They declined.** The proposal closes and the funding gap stays open.

Choose **Add later** to record an MoU or no-MoU answer without payments. The
row then shows **Add expected payments**, and **Budget attention** warns that a
partner agreed, because the funding gap is not correct yet. Saving an answer
never sends an email and never records money as received. Expected payments are
added only once for a proposal; edit them in **Payments** afterwards.

The row updates as soon as you save. If saving fails, the row returns to what
it was and the form reopens with your entries. Use **Change answer** to correct
an answer, or **Mark as waiting again** to clear it. Changing an answer does
not delete expected payments; delete those in **Payments** if the partner will
not pay.

Donations arrive through the giving platform. After an admin imports the CSV in
**Donations** and confirms the project, the money appears on the proposal row
under **Received**. Until then the row says the money shows after the next
Donations import. This is normal when imports happen only from time to time.

In **Cash flow**, money that is not an expected payment shows **Link to
proposal**. Choose the proposal it answers. **Remove link** clears it. Linking
changes no amounts.

A proposal with no recorded answer shows how many days it has waited. **Budget
attention** lists waiting proposals as information, not as a problem.

For one gift that covers several books, record **They will give a donation** on
each book’s proposal. For one signed agreement that covers several books, use a
**Shared MoU**.

## Funding partner & proposal

After saving the **Partner quote**, choose **Continue to partner proposals**. This
opens **Partners & proposals** without leaving the
budget page or clearing unfinished editors. Choose **Draft proposal** or
**Resume draft**, then review the recipient, CC, message, and attached quotation.
Confirm your review and choose **Send proposal** to send it. Continuing to the
email or drafting it never sends anything. If the funding email is missing, the
composer links back to **Funding partner** in **Quotation** so you can add it.
The **To** field follows the saved funding contact. Changing that email clears
your previous review confirmation, and sending stays disabled while it is missing.

The quote must finish saving before you can continue or download it. The
undercoverage warning does not block sharing a quote for part of the project.

Record the **funding partner / sponsor**, the contact's **first** and **last
name**, an **email**, and a free-text **contact note** in **Quotation settings**
— or **Use a saved partner** to fill them from the **Partners** directory
(Settings → Partners), which also links the project to that partner.
The email and first name address and greet the proposal below; the full name
appears on the quotation's **Attn:** line. The **Description of work** is
free-text so you can state the deliverable and format — e.g. "translated and
published in the target language", or "target-language eBook edition" — since it isn't always a print
run.

Committing an AI-reviewed agreement fills this card automatically when the
document identifies who is funding the work. That includes a license whose
counterparty is paying us; a normal license fee that we owe does not turn the
rights holder into a funding partner.

An older project can have a signed agreement but no saved funding partner.
**Partners & proposals** and **Payments** then show the partner named by the
signed agreement. Choose **Save as funding partner** to save it for this project
and add it to **Partners**. Sastra never replaces a partner you already chose.

Sastra does not create an invoice that has no partner. If you see **No funding
partner yet**, choose **Add funding partner** first. The invoice then shows who
it is for, and **Email invoice** fills in the partner's email for you to review.

If an invoice was created before the partner was known and has not been sent,
choose **Refresh invoice** on that payment. The invoice keeps its number
and amount. Review the new PDF before you send it.

The printing line is kept in step with the Print tab: in-app it carries a small
status badge (**quote** once a printer quote is accepted, otherwise **estimate**
/ **awaiting quote**); that status is not printed on the shared quotation.

## Early printer estimates for a budget

Already have prices? Choose **Add quote with AI** under **Estimated printing
cost**. Paste an email, printer quote, or planning note with copy quantities,
prices, and specifications. The printer is optional; leave it unspecified when
the source does not name one. Mark **Reused from another book** when appropriate.
Sastra also recognizes an explicit note about reusing a quote.

Choose **Read quote with AI**. The text stays available while Sastra reads it,
and after a failed attempt. Closing the intake also keeps your text. Each priced
copy quantity becomes a suggested planning option. Reused prices carry a
**Reused quote** label and a reminder to check that the specifications match.
Choose **View pasted source** to check the original note. This does not prepare
or send a printer email.

Review the options and choose **Use for budget estimate** on the quantity you
want. The existing partner quotation can then include that printing estimate
or its available quantity alternatives. Importing alone never chooses a price,
accepts a print order, creates a payment, or changes actual spend.

Managers can open **Estimated printing cost** on the main **Budget ▸ Quotation**
or **Print** tab for a proposed or active book project. Choose **Request estimate**,
select a printer with an email address, and enter the copy quantities to price.
The target-language page count starts from the budget's source pages or word
count and the project's language expansion factor; you can adjust it for this
request. Trim size comes from the project's Print settings.

An **English book PDF** is optional (up to 15 MB). Add text paper/color, cover
paper/finish, binding, or other specifications if known. The English PDF is a
reference: the request asks the printer to price the estimated target-language
pages. **Prepare email for review** saves a personal draft without sending.
Review the recipient, edit CC for this email, preview the attachment, and confirm
before **Send estimate request**. Save later edits with **Save draft**, or return
with **Resume draft**. Sending shows progress; a failed send retains your input.

Replies captured in Correspondence stay linked to the estimate request, even
if an actual print run has since been created. Prices from the email body or
an attached quote PDF become suggestions. You can also use **Add quote from text
or PDF** on the request. Review the source email, specifications, flagged fields,
copy count, total, and currency, then choose **Use for budget estimate**. The total
must be in the budget currency; Sastra does not convert currencies.

The selected price updates the main Print / Ship budget line as an **estimate**.
It creates no order, payment schedule, or actual spend. A later reviewed quote
for the actual first print run replaces it in the budget; merely creating a run
or receiving an unreviewed quote does not erase the estimate. Reprints keep
separate budgets. Actual spend still comes only from recorded paid print payments.
Select another tier to replace the estimate, or **Remove selected estimate** to
stop using it. The request and original quote remain in the history.

**Forwarded printer quotes also appear here.** If a printer quote reaches the
first print run, for example when you forward it to the project email, its copy
quantities appear under **Estimated printing cost** marked **From print run**.
You do not need to send an estimate request first. Check the details and choose
**Use for budget estimate** on one quantity. Sastra does not choose one for you.
The quote stays on the print run, and it shows **budget estimate** on the Print
tab. **Remove selected estimate** returns it to review. Review or reject it on
the Print tab. Accepting it there replaces the estimate as usual. If a print run
already has a reviewed quote, that quote sets the budget and these options are
hidden.

## Requesting internal approval

Budget approval is optional. If your team wants internal sign-off for a
quotation, a manager can set it up directly on the Budget screen. Approval is
separate for the main project and each reprint scope:

1. Select **Approvals** and open **Budget approval**.
2. Select one or more active workspace managers or admins directly on the Budget
   screen. They do not need a project coordinator role. The requester cannot
   select themself, and every selected person is required.
3. Choose the shared due date and click **Request approval**.

Until a request is created, proposals can be sent normally. Creating a request
activates the approval gate for that quotation. Each approver then receives an
assigned task and notification. The approval card shows who is waiting, who
approved, the time of each decision, and any requested change. Approval is
unanimous: one approval does not release the proposal while another person is
still waiting.

An approver opens the task or the Budget approval card and chooses **Approve**
or **Request changes**. A change request must explain what needs to be revised.
These tasks cannot be completed, reassigned, dragged to another status, or
deleted through ordinary task controls; only the dedicated decision records
approval. Managers and admins can change the due date from an approval task. For
the current round, this changes the shared deadline on every approver's task and
on the Budget approval card.

Changing a quotation line, quantity, unit price, total, currency, quotation
setting, funding partner, description of work, or saved partner quotation invalidates decisions for the
old version. Sastra preserves a completed round in **Approval history**, opens a
fresh round for the same approvers, and notifies anyone who must approve again.
Several edits made while a round is still pending are consolidated into that
round. Updating **Funding assigned**, **Spent**, or an internal budget note does not change
the partner quotation and does not invalidate approval.

Before a license or MoU is signed, managers can optionally set a **Proposed
completion date** on the proposal card. Leave it blank to draft and send without
mentioning a timeline. When set, drafted proposals offer that date as your
target timeline (instead of leaning on whatever date a publisher suggests). Once
an agreement sets a real completion deadline, that deadline governs and the
proposed date is no longer used — the card shows the agreement date instead.

**Suggest a date** opens a quick planner that proposes a realistic completion
date for a *new* project *on the same work path*. The new book can start once a
book currently **in production** frees a slot — or now, if a slot is free — and
then takes the typical duration. So the date stays **near-term** rather than
queuing years behind your whole backlog. Because it's scoped to the path, a
book's date isn't affected by the Creative media line (and vice-versa).

The planner shows the same reality as the Schedule roadmap: the path's committed
books **listed by due date**, with any that are **past their due date flagged
overdue (red)**. If the path already has overdue books, it says so plainly — the
suggested date assumes the new book takes the next open slot, so you'd be
prioritizing it over work that's already behind. Adjust the two assumptions
(projects at once, typical duration) to see the date move, then **Use this date**.
The defaults come from **Settings ▸ Workspace ▸ Planning capacity**, and a hint
shows what your completed books of this type actually averaged. It's a plain
calculation — nothing is sent anywhere.

**Draft proposal** (managers) writes an AI proposal email and opens a
**review-before-send composer** — nothing is sent until you review the recipient
and confirm. A draft can be created before a funding contact email is recorded;
the email is required only when sending. Drafting remains available before approval, but
once approval has been requested, **Send proposal** stays disabled until every
selected approver has approved the current quotation. Sending **attaches the
exact saved partner file**—sanitized Excel for itemized quotes or PDF for
price-per-copy quotes—and links the email to the project as
correspondence, so everything stays in
Sastra. Every proposal is kept in a **history** list (recipient, total, date,
the exact file that was sent) with the partner’s answer shown as a badge.
Record the answer under **Proposals and answers** (see **Record the partner’s
answer**). As with other emails, the drafter
learns from any edits you make. A funding contact email is required to send.

As soon as you confirm, proposal history adds a **Sending** row. When the mail
server succeeds, it becomes the canonical sent record; if delivery fails, the
temporary row disappears and the reviewed draft stays open. Proposal and
invoice composers also prefill the workspace default CC list, which remains
visible and editable for that one email.

A new draft uses **Funding proposal: Project title** as its default subject. It
states the amount and project first, identifies the attached quotation, includes
the proposed completion date only when one is available, and asks the partner
how they would like to proceed: sign an MoU, confirm by email, or give a
donation. It also invites changes. It does not assume an MoU. The generated sign-off uses your
name and the workspace organization. Editing either field in the composer is
safe: Sastra sends the exact version you review and confirm.

Use **Save draft** to keep proposal or invoice-email wording without sending.
The card shows the saved time and marks later edits as **Unsaved changes**.
After closing or refreshing, choose **Resume draft** / **Resume email** to
continue. A successful send removes your personal saved draft; a failed send
keeps it.

The review composer lists the exact quotation filename under **Attachments**.
Choose **Preview** before confirming. PDFs and images open inside Sastra;
spreadsheets show their exact filename and can be opened in a new tab for
inspection. Partner invoice emails use the same attachment review and show the
current invoice PDF that will be sent.

## Excel export

**Export to Excel** downloads the internal `.xlsx` that mirrors
the team's quotation layout with **live formulas**, so you can share it or edit it
outside the app. It carries your **workspace logo and accent color** (set in
Team settings → Branding), colored section bands, accounting number formats, and
a highlighted total. The tool costs every line, including Cover Design; if a
reference spreadsheet shows a lower total it's usually because a line was left
blank there — set a line to 0 if it genuinely shouldn't be charged.

The partner attachment is generated separately. Itemized mode includes only
lines marked visible and uses their partner labels and rates. Per-copy mode
creates a PDF with quantity, unit price, and total only. Neither file includes
internal line costs, internal notes, assigned-funding/spent values, or the organization
donation fee.

When you **accept a printer quote** on the Print tab, the print/ship line here is
updated to that quote's real total (including per-copy quotes that had no
explicit total), so the quotation and proposal reflect the confirmed print cost.

### Shared installment invoices

Shared agreement banners show each installment and its current readiness, alongside
this project's reviewed allocation. Open the agreement to manage the shared owner,
delivery evidence, and invoices. Generating a PDF does not complete the invoice
task: explicit reviewed sending completes it. The earliest invoice date is separate
from the partner's optional payment deadline. See **Shared MoU funding** for the
full review and delivery workflow.

The same send action completes a linked invoice task for a regular, single-project
expected payment. The task links back to its payment or shared agreement. Invoice
tasks cannot be marked done or deleted through ordinary task controls.

### Refresh an unsent invoice

Saving the project's **Default funding partner** also refreshes unsent generated
project invoices with the current saved partner name, contact email, and billing
address. Invoice numbers, amounts, currency, and dates stay the same. If refreshing
fails, the partner stays saved and a message tells you to use **Refresh invoice**.
That button also applies current document colors. Review the new PDF before sending.
A review of an older PDF cannot authorize sending a refreshed version.

Sent or uploaded invoices keep their original PDFs. Shared-agreement invoices and
separate selected-budget-line invoices keep their own reviewed recipient details;
changing the project's default partner does not reassign them. New invoice billing
partners, their contact emails, and billing addresses are also saved in Partners.

Choose invoice and quotation heading and table colors in **Settings → Workspace →
Invoice issuer → Invoice & quotation colors**. Colors apply to new or refreshed PDFs
and quotation exports.

### Edit a payment and its unsent invoice

Choose **Edit payment** on an unpaid row, or inside **Review invoice email**. The
same form includes the amount, trigger, expected date, owner, agreement/proposal
link, invoice description, and internal note. When an unsent generated invoice is
linked, you can also change its invoice date and due date.

Choose **Save payment & refresh invoice**. The payment, invoice PDF, and linked
task save together, retaining the invoice number and billing identity. Saving does
not send the invoice. Review the refreshed PDF before sending; the earlier PDF
review is cleared.
Your email draft stays available. A failed save keeps your entered fields open for
retry and leaves the previous financial records unchanged.

### Payment row actions

Each row shows the invoice or payment, its expected date and owner, its amount,
and a clear status such as **Ready to send**, **Sent**, or **Received**. Internal
scheduling notes stay visible below the summary; invoice-task assignment is shown
as supporting information.

**Review & send** (or **Resume email** for a saved draft), **Edit payment**, and
**Mark received** stay visible when applicable. Open **More** for **Refresh
invoice**, **Request invoice task**, **Upload paid invoice**, **Undo received
payment**, and destructive actions. Voiding and deletion still require confirmation.
Sent or received invoices retain their recorded PDF and financial terms.
