Chat is real-time messaging for the team. Every project has focused channels,
**#general** is shared with the whole workspace, selected teammates can
collaborate in private team channels, and two people can talk privately in a
one-to-one direct message.

Sent messages appear in the thread immediately with a **sending** or
**uploading** state. If delivery fails, the composer text and selected files are
restored so you can retry. Reactions, message pins, project pins, and confirmed
message deletes also update immediately and roll back if saving fails. Unread
counts clear as soon as you open a conversation.

## Getting around

Opening **Chat** returns to your last conversation that you can still access.
This includes direct messages, team channels, and project channels. If you have
no saved conversation, Chat shows the conversation list. On a phone, use
**Back** to choose another conversation, or **Conversation details → All chats**.

The chat sidebar lists **direct messages**, **team channels**, and active
projects. Completed and cancelled projects are kept out of this working list.
Use **Find a conversation** to filter the sidebar by teammate, team channel,
project, or project channel.

On a phone, the conversation list fits the screen. Long names are shortened so
the search field and project pin buttons stay visible.

Select the pin button beside a project (**Pin project**) to keep it at the top
of your sidebar; select it again (**Unpin project**) to let it move. Project
pins are only for you. Pinned projects stay anchored at the top. Unpinned
projects with unread messages
appear under **New activity**, ordered by the latest message, followed by
**Recent projects**. Unread names use stronger text and a count badge, and a
project shortcut opens its unread channel first. To keep large workspaces
scannable, the recent list starts with the latest projects; select **Show more**
to reveal the rest.

Use the plus button beside **Direct messages** to search for a teammate. Opening
the same teammate again returns to the existing private conversation.

Managers and admins can use the plus button beside **Team** to create a private
team channel. Give the channel a name and select its initial members; the
creator is included automatically. Only members can find the channel, open its
history, receive its mentions, or access files attached there.

Inside a private team channel, select the **members** button in the channel
header to view the roster. Managers and admins can add active teammates
immediately. Removing someone asks for confirmation, then removes the channel
from their sidebar and revokes access to its messages and files. The manager
making the change cannot remove themselves. Existing custom channels retain
their current audience when this feature is enabled, while **#general** remains
available to everyone.

When a conversation takes a moment to open, the message area shows **Loading
messages…** immediately while the conversation list and surrounding chat
workspace stay in place. The composer is temporarily unavailable so a message
cannot accidentally be sent to the previous channel.

On a phone, an open conversation stays within the visible screen like a
messaging app: the message history scrolls independently, while the compact
conversation header and composer remain reachable. The composer follows the
on-screen keyboard and leaves room for the device safe area. The conversation
uses one compact header. Select **Conversation details** for pins, members,
the assistant shortcut, theme, and workspace links. In a project's Chat tab,
**Back** returns to the project.

Nearby messages from the same teammate are grouped together. Day labels and
group times use your saved timezone. Your own messages appear on the right,
without repeating your name and photo. Select **Message actions** immediately
beside a bubble to reply, react, copy its text, pin, unpin, or delete that message.
The three-dot **More actions** button is closest to the bubble. On a computer,
hover over a bubble to reveal **Reply**, then **React** farther outward.
**Reply** goes straight to the composer; **React** opens your
quick emoji choices. You can also **right-click the bubble** to open all its
actions, including when some message text is highlighted. Right-clicking a link
keeps the browser's usual menu. You can still copy highlighted text with
**Ctrl+C** or **Command+C**.
Keyboard users can Tab to the controls, open **More actions** with Enter or
Space, or use **Shift+F10**. Use arrow keys to move through the menu, Enter to
choose, and Escape to close. Closing the menu returns focus to its control.

When there is room around the selected message, quick reactions appear above
it and the action list below. Near the edge of the conversation or on a short
screen, both stay together in a panel that fits the screen. Scrolling the
conversation closes the menu.

On a phone, **touch and hold a message** to open the same menu. The desktop
quick-action icons stay hidden; the three-dot button is also available.
Scrolling cancels the hold. Supported phones give a brief haptic tap when the
hold opens the menu; reduced motion disables it. Feedback depends on browser
and device support. The selected message and menu also provide visual feedback.
Choose a quick reaction or **More reactions** to
search all emoji, browse categories, and choose a skin tone. Choosing a reaction
closes the picker and updates the message immediately. Your reactions are
highlighted in the menu and reaction details; select one again to remove it.
Failed changes roll back with an error. You can delete your own messages after
confirmation.

Messages show one compact **reaction summary** slightly over the bubble's lower
edge: up to three popular emoji and the total number of reactions. Tap it
(**View reactions**) to see every emoji and its count. In these details, your
reactions are marked **Yours**; tap an emoji to add or remove your reaction.
Opening the summary only views the details and does not change a reaction.

Quick reactions and the full picker's **Frequently used** row prioritize the
emoji you have used most in saved reactions across your chats, with recent use
breaking ties. This is personal to your account and works across devices.
Until you have a history, suggestions include **👍 ❤️ 🙏 😂 🎉 ✅ 👀 🙌**.
Unused slots keep these defaults, and your most-used skin tone is preserved.
On very short screens, the full picker makes more room for search results;
your personal quick reactions remain available in Message actions.
Removing a reaction or deleting its message removes that use from the history.

## Editing and deleting your messages

Open **Message actions** beside one of your sent text messages, or touch and
hold it on a phone, then choose **Edit message**. The composer switches to
**Editing message**. Make your correction and select **Save changes**, or use
**Cancel edit** (or **Escape**) to leave the message unchanged. Your normal
new-message draft, selected files, and recorded voice draft stay separate and
return when you finish editing.

Saved corrections appear immediately for everyone and show **Edited** beside
the original message time. Existing attachments, reactions, and pins stay with
the message. You can correct the text accompanying attachments; this does not
replace the attached files. Blank edits are not saved. If a save fails, the
original text returns and your correction stays in the editor so you can retry.
If another session changed the message first, cancel and reopen Edit to review
the latest version before saving again.

Choose **Delete message** to remove your message for everyone in that
conversation. Confirm before it disappears. Its shared pin is removed too;
failed deletions restore the message. Other people's messages can be copied,
reacted to, or pinned, but you cannot edit or delete them.

Corrections do not send a fresh chat or mention notification. Editing or
deleting chat text does not resubmit a standup answer or rewrite saved standup
reports.

On a touch device, **Return** adds a new line; select **Send** to send.
On desktop, **Enter** sends and **Shift+Enter** adds a new line.
**Ctrl+Enter** or **Command+Enter** also sends. Choosing a mention or confirming
text in another language does not send the message.

On a short screen, such as a phone turned sideways, the project header, tabs,
and channel tabs can use most of the height. The project chat area then scrolls
on its own, so you can scroll down to the message box and **Send**. Your draft
stays in place.

## Replying to a message

Select **Message actions** (the three dots beside a message), then **Reply**.
The composer shows who you are replying to and a preview of their message, and
the message field is focused so you can start typing immediately.
Write your reply, attach files or record audio if needed, then select **Send**.
Use **Cancel reply** to send a regular message instead; your text and attachments
stay in the composer. Selecting Reply does not send anything.

Sent replies include a quoted preview of the original message. Select the quote
to scroll to and briefly highlight the original when it is in the recent messages.
Older originals still have a quoted preview, but cannot be scrolled to from the
recent thread. Attachment-only originals show the file name or **Voice message**.
If the original is deleted, the quote shows **Original message unavailable**
instead of its content.

Replies work in project channels, team channels, and direct messages. A failed
send restores both your draft and its reply context so you can retry. Replies use
the conversation's existing notifications; replying does not automatically
mention the original author. Use **@mention** when you need to notify them.

## In a channel

- **@mention** a teammate to notify them: type **@**, pick a name from the
  picker, and they get a notification linking back to your message. See
  **@mentions**.
- Attach files inline, add **reactions**, and **pin** important messages so
  everyone can find them again. See **Pinned messages** below.
- **Drag files into the conversation** or select **Attach files** to add them
  to your draft. You can drop several files, including PowerPoint presentations
  (`.ppt` and `.pptx`), documents, images, and videos. Each sent file can be up
  to 50 MB. Videos can start larger; see **Sending videos** below.
  Review the file names, add a message, or remove files before selecting
  **Send**. Dropping files does not upload or send them. Files cannot be added
  while messages are loading, an upload is running, or you are recording audio.
- **Paste an image or screenshot** into the message box, or select **Attach
  files**. Images appear as previews before sending. You can add text or remove
  an image, then select **Send** to upload it. Sending shows progress; failed
  uploads stay in the composer for retry. Sent images appear directly in the
  conversation, with larger previews when space allows. Small images keep their
  original size, and portrait images fit without cropping. Use the download
  button to save a copy. If a preview cannot load, the file remains available
  to open or download.
- Select the **microphone** to record a voice message of up to ten minutes.
  Stop the recording to review it, listen before sending, record it again, or
  discard it. You can add a short note or transcript before sending so the
  message is useful without audio. Recordings are compressed for speech before
  upload, voice uploads show real progress, and failed uploads remain available
  to retry. Sent voice messages use a simple play button, waveform scrubber,
  and elapsed time directly in the conversation.
- Read tracking shows what's new since you last looked.

Direct messages use the same composer, attachments, voice recordings,
reactions, and read tracking as channels. Only the two participants can open
the thread or its attached files; add/remove member controls apply to private
team channels rather than turning a direct message into a group conversation.

Voice recordings are stored as private workspace file attachments. Sastra
serves playback and downloads through authenticated links rather than exposing
the storage object directly.

Standups also use chat. At the scheduled time, the standup bot opens your
standup conversation and asks the first question. You get a notification, and
the **Standups** page shows **Your standup is waiting** with **Answer now**.
Reply in that conversation: each reply saves your answer, and the bot asks the
next question until the check-in is done. Standup conversations are not listed
in the chat sidebar. Your standup conversation is private: only you can open it,
along with its files and pins. Your answers are not posted to a team channel;
they feed the standup summary on the **Standups** page.

Creating channels and changing their membership are manager actions.

## Sending videos

Select **Attach files** or drag a video into the conversation. Add a short
description so others can understand your message without sound.

Choose **Video quality** before selecting **Send**:

- **Standard · smaller file** makes a smaller video, up to 720p.
- **Higher quality · clearer text** keeps more detail, up to 1080p. Use this
  for screen recordings where people need to read text.
- **Original · up to 50 MB** sends an MP4 or MOV without compression. Use this
  if your browser cannot prepare the video. Some devices may need to download
  the original to play it.

Videos that are already suitable for chat keep their original quality. Sastra
checks the file size, picture size, frame rate, and format before compressing.
Compatible MP4 files skip compression when another encode is unlikely to make
them much smaller. Small compatible MP4 files also skip compression. Other
compatible formats may be repackaged as MP4 without changing picture or sound.
Videos that are too large or use an incompatible format still need preparation.

Standard and Higher quality accept videos up to 500 MB and ten minutes. Sastra
makes a smaller MP4 on your device after **Send**, before uploading it. Longer
videos may use a lower resolution to fit the 50 MB upload limit. Your original
file is not changed. Preparation speed depends on your device and browser.
Keep this tab open while the video is being prepared and uploaded.

You see preparation progress followed by upload progress. **Cancel preparation**
stops local processing and restores your message and selected files. No message
is sent if preparation fails or is cancelled. Failed uploads keep their files
in the composer for retry. If the video is still too large, shorten it or share
a link instead. Sastra never removes sound just to finish a conversion.

Sent videos have playback controls directly in the conversation. They do not
play automatically. Use **Download** to save a copy. If playback fails, select
**Reload video** or download it. Videos use the conversation's existing private
file permissions, including direct messages and standup conversations.

## Viewing images

Select a sent image to open the **image viewer** without leaving the chat.
Use the zoom buttons, or **pinch** or **double-tap** on a phone. On a computer,
scroll or double-click to zoom. Drag a zoomed image to read another part.
Zooming affects the image only; it does not change the saved file.

If a message has several images, swipe between them or use the previous and
next buttons. The viewer shows the filename and your position in that message.
It only browses images from that message. A single image has no navigation
arrows.

Keyboard users can use **+** and **−** to zoom. Arrow keys change images at
normal size and pan when zoomed. **Escape** or **Close image viewer** closes
the viewer and returns focus to the image in chat. On a phone, you can also
swipe down to close an image at normal size.

The download button saves the image currently shown. If an image cannot load,
use **Open original image** or download it. If the viewer itself cannot load,
you can cancel, reload the page, or open the original image.

## Pinned messages

Pin a message when the whole conversation should be able to find it again, for
example a decision, a final file, or an important date.

- **Pin a message:** open **Message actions** beside the message, then select
  **Pin message**. On a phone, you can also touch and hold the message to open
  these actions.
- A pinned message shows **Pinned by** and the teammate's name, or **Pinned by
  you**.
- **Unpin a message:** select the same button again (**Unpin message**), or use
  **Unpin message** in the Pinned list.
- **See all pins:** select **Pinned** in the conversation header. The number
  beside it shows how many messages are pinned. The newest pin is first. The
  list keeps the newest 50 pins; when it is full, the number shows **50+**. Each
  pin shows who wrote the message, when it was sent, the start of its text (or
  **Voice message** or the file name), and who pinned it and when. The
  **Pinned** button is in the header of every conversation in **Chat** and on a
  project's **Chat** tab.
- **Go to a pinned message:** select **Show in chat** (or the message in the
  list). The chat scrolls to the message and highlights it for a moment. A pin
  on an older message that is not in the recent messages stays in the list, but
  the chat cannot scroll to it.

Pins are shared with everyone in the conversation. Anyone who can read a
conversation can pin or unpin its messages: project channels, **#general**,
private team channels you belong to, and your direct messages. If you are
removed from a private team channel, you can no longer see or change its pins.
Deleting a pinned message also removes its pin. Pinning and unpinning do not
send notifications.

The assistant can list pinned messages, for example "what is pinned in
#general?". It can also unpin a message after you approve it. It cannot pin
messages for you; use the pin button on the message.

## Assistant shortcut

In a conversation, use the assistant shortcut in the header or in **Conversation details** on a phone. The assistant launcher does not float over Send. Project chat keeps the conversation and composer inside the available screen height.

On a phone, open **Conversation details** to find the **Pinned** button and
private channel membership controls.

## On wide screens

On a wide screen, messages and the message box stay in a centered column of
comfortable width, so replies are easy to follow. The channel list stays on the
left.
