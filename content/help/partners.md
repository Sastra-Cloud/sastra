**Partners** (Settings → Partners) is a reusable directory of funding partners
and MoU counterparties — foundations, churches, sponsors — much like
**Publishers** is for rights holders. Managers manage it.

## Organizations and contacts

Add a partner with a name and optional website / notes. Each organization can
hold **several contacts**, because you often deal with different people at the
same org. A contact has a **first and last name**, **email**, optional **phone**
and **role**, and one can be marked the **primary** contact. Add, edit, and
remove contacts inline; deleting an organization removes its contacts.

## Filled in by document import

When you import a document (MoU, proposal, agreement) that names a sponsor or
counterparty funding the work, the intake **creates or updates the matching
Partner** and adds the contact it found — so the directory fills itself over
time. A license counterparty is included when the agreement has inbound
payments to us, but not when the license only records a fee we owe. Existing
partners are matched by name (case-insensitive) and contacts are de-duplicated,
so re-importing won't make copies.

## Using a partner on a quotation

In a project's **Quotation settings**, **Use a saved partner** lets you pick a
Partner and one of its contacts to fill the funding partner / sponsor, contact
name, and email. The project remembers which Partner it's linked to, and a
document import links it automatically. You can still edit the funding fields by
hand afterward. From there the branded quotation and the **funding proposal**
email address that partner.
