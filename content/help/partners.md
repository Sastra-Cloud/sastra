**Partners** (Settings → Partners) is a reusable directory of funding partners
and MoU counterparties — foundations, churches, sponsors — much like
**Publishers** is for rights holders. Managers manage it.

## Organizations and contacts

Add a partner with a name, optional website, **billing address**, and notes.
You can enter its first contact's **first name**, **last name**, **email**,
**phone**, and **role** in the same form; that contact becomes primary.
The billing address is used on new invoices. Each organization can
hold **several contacts**, because you often deal with different people at the
same org. A contact has a **first and last name**, **email**, optional **phone**
and **role**, and one can be marked the **primary** contact. Add, edit, and
remove contacts inline; deleting an organization removes its contacts.

## Filled in by document import

When you import a document (MoU, proposal, agreement) that names a sponsor or
counterparty funding the work, the intake **creates or updates the matching
Partner** and adds the contact it found — so the directory fills itself over
time. A license counterparty is included when the agreement has inbound
payments to us, but not when the license only records a fee we owe. Existing
partners are matched by name (case-insensitive) and contacts are de-duplicated,
so re-importing won't make copies.

## Using a partner on a quotation

In a project's **Quotation settings**, **Use a saved partner** lets you pick a
Partner and one of its contacts to fill the funding partner / sponsor, contact
name, and email. The project remembers which Partner it's linked to, and a
document import links it automatically. You can still edit the funding fields by
hand afterward. From there the branded quotation and the **funding proposal**
email address that partner.

Entering a partner manually in **Default funding partner** also saves the partner
and contact to the directory. **New → Add & use** lets you include its website,
billing address, notes, phone, and contact role at the same time. The project link
and directory details save together; a failed save keeps your entered details.

In **Partners & proposals**, entering a new partner name and saving the quotation
also adds that partner to the directory. Entered contact details are saved too,
and the first contact becomes primary. The partner is then available in **Choose
a saved partner** on future quotations. Existing names are matched without regard
to letter case, and contact emails are matched within the chosen partner. Changing
the partner name on a quotation does not rename an existing directory entry.
If the quotation cannot be saved, the new partner and contact are not added.

Saving a **Sponsorship** draft also saves its bill-to name, contact email, and
billing address as a reusable partner for future proposals and invoices.
These saves reuse partner names regardless of letter case and surrounding spaces.
They fill missing directory details and preserve existing addresses and contact
details. To change a saved directory entry, edit it in **Settings → Partners**.

Generating a project or selected-budget-line invoice also saves its billing partner,
contact email, and address to the directory. Saving the project's default funding
partner refreshes eligible unsent project invoices with that partner's saved address.
