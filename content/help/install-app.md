Sastra runs in your browser but can also be **installed** like an app, so it
opens from your Home Screen and can send **push notifications** — task
assignments, @mentions, and blocker alerts — even when it's closed.

You'll see a **Turn on notifications** card on the **Home** when this device
can still be set up. You can dismiss it (it stays hidden for 30 days) and set
things up any time from **Settings ▸ Notifications**.

## On Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Settings ▸ Notifications** and tap **Turn on notifications**, then
   **Allow** when the browser asks. That's enough to start receiving push.
2. Optional: tap **Install app** (when offered) to add Sastra to your Home
   Screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

## On iPhone or iPad (Safari)

Apple only lets apps saved to the **Home Screen** send notifications, so there's
one extra step. You need **iOS 16.4 or later**.

1. In **Safari**, tap the **Share** icon (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on
   iPad).
2. Scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Sastra** from your Home Screen, go to **Settings ▸ Notifications**, and
   tap **Turn on notifications** → **Allow**.

The settings page walks you through these steps and the Home card has a
**Show me how** guide.

## Confirming it works

Once a device is registered you'll see it under **Devices receiving push** in
**Settings ▸ Notifications**, along with a **Send test notification** button. The
test always goes through, even during quiet hours, so you can be sure delivery is
working on that phone or computer.

You can remove a device from that list if you stop using it — it simply stops
receiving push until you turn notifications on again from that device.

## If notifications are blocked

If you previously tapped **Block**, the app can't ask again. Turn them back on in
your device settings:

- **iPhone/iPad:** Settings app → **Notifications** → **Sastra** → **Allow
  Notifications**.
- **Android / desktop:** tap the lock (or tune) icon next to the address bar →
  **Permissions** → allow **Notifications**.

Then return to **Settings ▸ Notifications** and tap **Check again**.

## Choosing when push arrives

Web-push respects the **quiet hours**, **work hours**, **active-only**, and
**pause** controls on the same settings page. See *Notifications* for details.
Installing the app also lets Sastra show a simple **You're offline** screen when
your connection drops.
