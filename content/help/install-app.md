Sastra runs in your browser but can also be **installed** like an app, so it
opens from your Dock, app launcher, or Home Screen and can send **push notifications** — task
assignments, @mentions, and blocker alerts — even when it's closed.

You'll see a **Turn on notifications** card on the **Home** when this device
can still be set up. You can dismiss it (it stays hidden for 30 days) and set
things up any time from **Settings ▸ Notifications**.

## On Mac (Safari)

You need **macOS Sonoma 14 or later** to install a Safari web app.
Safari uses **Add to Dock** for installation. The **Add to Dock** button in
Sastra opens a guide; Safari's own menu completes the installation.

1. In Safari's menu bar, choose **File → Add to Dock**. You can also choose
   **Add to Dock** from Safari's **Share** menu.
2. Keep the name **Sastra** and click **Add**.
3. Open **Sastra** from your Dock. Sign in if asked.
4. In the Dock app, open **Settings ▸ Notifications** and click
   **Turn on notifications** → **Allow**.

The Dock app and Safari have separate notification settings. Turn notifications
on inside the Dock app, even if you already allowed them in Safari.
The Dock icon can then show your unread notification count.

You can also receive notifications in Safari without installing the app.
If **Add to Dock** is missing from Safari's menu, check your macOS version.

## Downloading files in the app

File downloads keep your current Sastra page open. Use the browser or app's
Downloads button to open the saved file. Invoice previews open the document
itself. In email attachment review, **Download attachment** saves files that
cannot be previewed, such as Excel workbooks.

If an older version left an empty window after a download, close that window
and open Sastra from the Dock again. The downloaded file remains in Downloads.

## On desktop (Edge or Chrome)

Click **Install app** when offered, or use your browser's install option.
You can also turn on notifications without installing Sastra. Installation
is still available in Sastra's notification settings after notifications are on.

## On Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Settings ▸ Notifications** and tap **Turn on notifications**, then
   **Allow** when the browser asks. That's enough to start receiving push.
2. Optional: tap **Install app** (when offered) to add Sastra to your Home
   Screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

## On iPhone or iPad (Safari)

Apple only lets apps saved to the **Home Screen** send notifications, so there's
one extra step. You need **iOS 16.4 or later**.

1. In **Safari**, tap the **Share** icon (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on
   iPad).
2. Scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Sastra** from your Home Screen, go to **Settings ▸ Notifications**, and
   tap **Turn on notifications** → **Allow**.

The settings page walks you through these steps and the Home card has a
**Show me how** guide.

## Confirming it works

Once a device is registered you'll see it under **Devices receiving push** in
**Settings ▸ Notifications**, along with a **Send test notification** button. The
test always goes through, even during quiet hours, so you can be sure delivery is
working on that phone or computer. Open the installed app to register it before
testing. If a Mac test does not appear, check **System Settings → Notifications
→ Sastra** and your **Focus** settings.

You can remove a device from that list if you stop using it — it simply stops
receiving push until you turn notifications on again from that device.

## If notifications are blocked

If you previously tapped **Block**, the app can't ask again. Turn them back on in
your device settings:

- **iPhone/iPad:** Settings app → **Notifications** → **Sastra** → **Allow
  Notifications**.
- **Mac Dock app:** System Settings → **Notifications** → **Sastra** → turn on
  **Allow notifications**.
- **Safari tab on Mac:** Safari → **Settings** → **Websites** →
  **Notifications** → allow this site. Also check **System Settings →
  Notifications** and **Focus** if alerts do not appear.
- **Android / Edge / Chrome:** tap the lock (or tune) icon next to the address bar →
  **Permissions** → allow **Notifications**.

Then return to **Settings ▸ Notifications** and tap **Check again**.

## Choosing when push arrives

Web-push respects the **quiet hours**, **work hours**, **active-only**, and
**pause** controls on the same settings page. See *Notifications* for details.
Installing the app also lets Sastra show a simple **You're offline** screen when
your connection drops.

See [Apple's Mac web app guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104996) for
Safari's installation and notification settings.
