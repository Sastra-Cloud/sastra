Two surfaces answer *what should I do next?* — the **Home** is your home
base, while **My Work** is where you execute, track, and review your workday.

## Home

Your Home is the first thing you see. It shows:

- **Do in this order** immediately after the greeting, with your three most
  important next tasks. Complete them in place or open **My Work** for the full
  queue.
- A compact **You're caught up** message when nothing needs attention. Future
  assigned work stays under **Later** in My Work until it is within 30 days or
  someone starts it.
- For managers and admins, **Needs your review** combines actionable suggestions
  from project updates and correspondence. Each item opens the project or email
  where its evidence and review-first actions are available; no project data is
  changed from the Home itself.
- **Active projects** with health dots, progress, and a one-line preview of each
  project's latest status update, including who posted it and when. Book rows
  also show a funding summary based on recorded budgets, promised funding,
  receipts, and any explicit print-funding decision. Projects without an update
  are clearly marked. The list starts with the newest
  meaningful project activity. Posting or editing a project status update,
  changing project-level details such as its status or schedule, or processing
  linked inbound email moves the project up. Replies, chat, routine task changes,
  outbound email, and unlinked mailbox email do not affect the order.
- Only the work-status cards that currently contain something: **Assigned to
  me**, **Overdue**, and **Due this week**. When all three are zero, one quiet
  all-clear message replaces the cards.
- Notification setup after the work content, when this device can still enable
  it.

If you're active on more projects than is healthy for your team size, the
Home warns you so work doesn't get spread too thin.

## My Work

Open **My Work** from the **Today** navigation and choose:

- **Focus** for the work you are doing now, what needs attention next, and what
  you completed today.
- **Board** to move your assigned tasks through To do, In progress, Review, and
  Done.
- **Agenda** for the date-ordered operational schedule.

The former standalone Agenda link now opens the Agenda view inside My Work.

## Agenda

The **Agenda** view gathers everything with a date attached to you — tasks,
payments, project deadlines, and rights deadlines — into four buckets:
**Overdue**, **This week**, **Next two weeks**, and **Later**. Use the filter to
focus on your tasks, deadlines, rights, or finance without opening each project.
