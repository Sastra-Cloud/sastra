## Enable the module

An admin can open **Settings → Modules** and enable **Sponsorships** for the workspace.
It is off by default. Managers and admins see Sponsorships in the main menu when
enabled. Members cannot open sponsorship records or their invoice files.

Disabling the module hides its screens and blocks its actions. It keeps all
invoices, payments, and use history. Enable it again to continue.

## Create a sponsorship

Open **Sponsorships → Create sponsorship**. Choose a saved partner or enter an
individual's or organization's bill-to name. A contact email is optional until
you send the invoice. Saved partners fill their billing address and contact email;
you can change these for this sponsorship.

In each **Book** field, type part of a title to search, then choose a matching
book project. You can also open the list to browse, or use the arrow keys and
Enter to choose a book. Add the number of sponsored copies and agreed price per
copy. Review the invoice description for each book. One invoice can cover several
books. Prices are sponsorship prices; enter the amount agreed with the partner.
Sponsored quantities do not record copies distributed or change print quantities.

Add an optional payment due date and invoice note. The note appears on the invoice.
New sponsorships use the workspace currency and snapshot its organization donation
fee. Existing sponsorships keep their saved currency and fee after Settings changes.

Create and edit drafts before issuing an invoice. A cancelled draft stays in history.

## Generate and send an invoice

Review the bill-to name, books, prices, and total, then confirm the review and
choose **Generate invoice**. Invoice numbers, issuer identity, logo, and payment
instructions come from the same workspace settings as other Sastra invoices.
Complete missing issuer details under **Settings → Workspace**.

The saved PDF includes book descriptions, quantities, prices, and totals. Preview
it before sending. **Review and send invoice** opens the email with visible To and
CC fields. Edit the message and CC list. **Save draft** keeps an unfinished email.
Check the send confirmation, then choose **Confirm and send invoice**.

Generating the PDF does not send an email. An unresolved delivery blocks another
send; check Correspondence first. Invoice emails are linked to all covered book
projects. Issued invoices keep their original PDF. To change an invoice, void it,
edit the returned draft, and generate a replacement. Previous invoices stay in
history. Reverse any received payments before voiding an invoice.

## Record funding received

On an issued sponsorship, choose **Record funding received**. Review the gross
amount, actual amount available after fees, date, and optional payment note.
The saved organization donation fee suggests the available amount; edit it to
match the actual funds received.

You can record part of a payment and add the rest later. The payment is allocated
across the books' remaining invoice shares. Allocations add up exactly, including
odd cents. The invoice is paid when its full gross amount has been recorded.

## Use sponsorship funds bit by bit

Each book has its own **Funds received**, **Used**, and **Available** balance.
These funds are separate from the project budget and its funding totals. Nothing
is automatically added to a budget, print run, or expense.

Choose **Record funds used** for a book. Enter the amount used, the date, and what
it paid for, then review and confirm. You can record any part of the available
balance. For example, USD 500 received and USD 125 used leaves USD 375 available.
Several small uses can be recorded over time. You cannot use more than the funds
available for that book under that sponsorship.

Recording use tracks what happened; it does not send money or create a budget
expense. A book's project overview shows these sponsorship balances and links to
the sponsorship where you can record more use. Different currencies stay separate.

## Correct a payment or use

Choose **Correct payment** or **Correct use** in Funding history. Enter a reason
and confirm the reversal. The original entry and reason stay visible. Then record
the corrected entry. Reversing a use restores the available balance.

A payment cannot be reversed if that would leave a book's received funds below
the amount already used. Reverse the related uses first. Repeated saves of the
same request do not add another payment or use.
