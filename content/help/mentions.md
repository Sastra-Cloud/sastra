You can **tag a teammate** anywhere you write to the team by typing **@** and
their name. They get a notification that links straight back to what you wrote.

## How to mention someone

1. Start typing **@** in any comment or message box.
2. A picker appears listing teammates — keep typing to filter, or use the
   **arrow keys**.
3. Press **Enter** (or click a name) to insert the mention.

Mentioned names are highlighted in the posted message, and your own mentions are
highlighted for you so tagged messages are easy to spot.

## Where mentions work

- **Chat** — project and team channels.
- **Task comments** — in the task details dialog.
- **Project status updates** and their **replies**.
- **Budget notes** and **Rights notes** on a project.

In a project, the picker suggests that project's **members** and **managers**; in
a team channel it suggests everyone on the team.

## What the person gets

A mention creates a **notification** (the bell), a **web-push** alert, and — if
they've turned on Workflow emails — an **email**. Each one links back to the
message. Mention emails are queued right away instead of waiting for a bundle or
daily digest. Push notifications follow your **quiet hours** or **work hours**;
see **Notifications**. You are never notified for mentioning yourself.
