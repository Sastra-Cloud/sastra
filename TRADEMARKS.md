## What you may do without asking

- Run an unmodified Sastra release for your own organization and call it

- Say truthfully that your installation "runs Sastra" or is "based on Sastra",

- Use the name in blog posts, talks, documentation, and comparisons.
- Link to sastra.cloud and to the public repository.

- Using the logo, or a confusingly similar name or logo, for a modified version.
- Registering domains, social accounts, or company names that include "Sastra"
  in connection with software or publishing tools.

## Modified versions

If you distribute a modified Sastra, please pick a different name and logo so
people can tell it apart from the releases published at
github.com/Sastra-Cloud/sastra. Keeping the AGPL source link and the notice
"based on Sastra" is welcome.
